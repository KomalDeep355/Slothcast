NOT JUST VIBES. ACTUAL RESEARCH.

The science behind tiny steps.

Slothcast borrows ideas from procrastination and goal-setting research. These strategies are evidence-informed, not guaranteed treatments.

✳
01 / EMOTIONS♡
☁ → ☀

Make the task feel less awful.

Procrastination can offer short-term emotional relief. Noticing the barrier can help you choose a more manageable way to begin.

Sirois & Pychyl (2013) ↗ Procrastination and emotion regulation
02 / IF–THEN PLANS✎
IF ✳ THEN →

Give your intention a trigger.

An if–then plan links a specific situation to an action, making it easier to translate intentions into behavior.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006) ↗ Implementation intentions meta-analysis
03 / INTERVENTIONS✿
little by little

Find an approach that fits.

Reviews of procrastination interventions support studying practical approaches while recognizing that effects vary across people and studies.

Turner & Hodis (2023) ↗ Systematic review of academic interventions

Slothcast is a self-help and task-planning prototype, not a diagnostic tool or a replacement for professional care. Research findings do not guarantee that a particular strategy will work for every person.
